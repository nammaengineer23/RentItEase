RentItEase visual asset manifest

Runtime Flutter assets use SVG for logos, icons, onboarding illustrations,
and empty/property-placeholder artwork.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
branding/google_g.svg
branding/rentitease_logo_512x512.svg

onboarding/easy_bookings.svg
onboarding/secure_payments.svg
properties/property_placeholder.svg

PROPERTY PHOTOS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Listing photographs are genuine user/owner uploads delivered by the API.
The app does not bundle fake property photography. If a listing has no valid
photo, properties/property_placeholder.svg is displayed.

PLATFORM EXCEPTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Android launcher resources, iOS AppIcon/LaunchImage resources, and web/PWA
icons remain PNG in their platform directories. Those operating-system asset
catalogs require raster outputs; their editable branding source is the
RentItEase SVG logo above.
