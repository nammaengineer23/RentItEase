# Support and Privacy Operations

## Support

Public support contact: `support@rentitease.com`.

Ask users for their registered email or phone number, relevant record ID, device/app version, time of the issue, and a screenshot. Never ask for passwords, OTPs, JWTs, or Razorpay secrets.

## Account deletion

Users can request deletion through `https://rentitease.com/delete-account` by emailing support from their registered address. Verify identity before processing. Delete or anonymize eligible account data while retaining only records required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or regulatory obligations.

## Public legal URLs

Privacy Policy, Terms, Contact, About, and Delete Account pages must stay public and require no sign-in.
